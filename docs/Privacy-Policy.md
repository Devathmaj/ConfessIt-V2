# ConfessIt Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 26, 2026**

ConfessIt is an anonymous social and campus-engagement platform operated by an individual developer. This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed when you use ConfessIt, why it is processed, how content moderation works, and how to contact the operator about your information.

**Privacy contact:** [devathmaj@gmail.com](mailto:devathmaj@gmail.com)

## 1. Scope and operator

ConfessIt may be provided directly or through a school, college, or another organisation. An organisation's administrator may create or provision accounts, provide account details, and configure which classes or groups can participate in matchmaking. That organisation may also have its own policies and responsibilities. Contact the organisation that provided your account for questions about its account-provisioning or administrative decisions, and contact ConfessIt for questions about the service itself.

ConfessIt is primarily operated from India and may be accessed from other countries. The privacy rights that apply to you may depend on where you live and the circumstances in which the service is provided.

## 2. Information we process

Depending on how you use ConfessIt, we may process the following information:

- **Account and identity information:** email address used for magic-link authentication, registration number (used as a username or identifier), full name, class, branch, division, and gender. ConfessIt does not use a password-based sign-in flow.
- **Profile information:** profile pictures you upload.
- **Content you submit or create:** confessions, comments, love notes, chat messages, profile images, reports, and information generated by your interactions with games or other features.
- **Security and technical information:** IP address, browser or user-agent details, and last-login time. These are used for account security, abuse prevention, rate limiting, and protection against denial-of-service attacks.
- **Moderation and safety records:** submitted reports, moderation outcomes, and information needed to investigate abuse, apply account restrictions, or maintain platform safety.
- **Search data:** a vector embedding generated from confession text for semantic search. The embeddings are stored with the confession data in the ConfessIt database and are used to find semantically relevant confessions for users and administrators.
- **Operational logs:** ConfessIt logs operational and security events. User-identifying information in logs is hashed where implemented. Hashing does not necessarily make data anonymous or impossible to link back to the same account or event.

Accounts may be provisioned by an organisation's administrator rather than created solely by the person who ultimately uses the account. Please contact that organisation if you believe it supplied incorrect account information.

## 3. How we use information

We use information to:

- authenticate users through email magic links and operate user accounts;
- provide confessions, comments, matchmaking, time-limited chats, love notes, games, leaderboards, and administrative features;
- configure and enforce the matchmaking groups selected by the relevant administrator;
- create and search confession embeddings;
- inspect submissions for potentially harmful or inappropriate content, handle reports, and allow moderators or administrators to review moderation decisions;
- redact personally identifiable information from the version of a love note presented for moderation;
- send account-related and service emails;
- detect misuse, spam, suspicious access, denial-of-service activity, and security incidents; and
- maintain, debug, protect, and improve the service.

ConfessIt does not currently use third-party advertising trackers or advertising cookies, and it does not operate paid features.

## 4. Anonymous content is not anonymous to the platform

Confessions are presented anonymously to ordinary users: the application does not display the author's identity alongside a confession. This is **not absolute anonymity**. The database can associate a confession with its author, and when a confession is reported, its author identity is revealed on the admin side for review. Author information may also be accessible to authorised administrators through related records during an investigation. Information you include in the content itself may also reveal your identity.

Do not submit information on the assumption that ConfessIt administrators or the underlying service infrastructure can never associate it with you.

## 5. Automated moderation and human review

ConfessIt uses automated moderation to help detect potentially harmful or inappropriate material. Automated systems can make mistakes and may miss material that should have been flagged.

- **Confessions, comments, and chats:** content is checked using Azure AI Content Safety. When content is flagged or reported, it may be hidden from the application and a report may be made available to administrators or moderators. They can review the report and decide whether to reinstate the content or leave it hidden.
- **Profile pictures:** uploaded images are checked using Azure AI Content Safety. Images that are rejected cannot be reinstated through the current workflow; a user must submit a different image.
- **Love notes:** Azure AI Language is used to identify and redact personally identifiable information before a note is shown in the moderator/admin review interface. The original love-note text remains in the database. Redaction of the review copy does **not** mean the original text has been erased; it may be accessible through direct inspection of the underlying database by someone with suitable technical access.

Users are notified when relevant content is removed. ConfessIt does not currently provide an in-app appeal workflow. You may contact [devathmaj@gmail.com](mailto:devathmaj@gmail.com) to raise a concern about moderation or account action.

## 6. AI services and other service providers

ConfessIt relies on infrastructure and service providers to operate features. Depending on the feature, information may be processed by the following providers:

- **Microsoft Azure AI Content Safety:** receives submitted confessions, comments, chat text, and images for content-safety analysis.
- **Azure AI Language:** receives love-note text for personally identifiable information detection and redaction.
- **Microsoft Foundry embedding model:** processes confession text to generate embeddings for semantic search. The resulting embeddings are stored in the Azure Database for PostgreSQL used by ConfessIt.
- **Microsoft Foundry and, at times, Groq:** used to generate game material for Case Closed and Plot Twist, such as story content, choices, outcomes, and related game configuration. The game-generation prompts do not include player chats, account details, profile information, or other player-specific information.
- **Azure hosting, database, functions, storage, and email services:** used to host and operate the application, persist data, process background tasks, and deliver service emails. Azure Communication Services Email receives the information needed to deliver an email, including the recipient address and message content.
- **Cloudinary:** stores and delivers uploaded profile pictures and love-note images.

These providers process information as needed to deliver the relevant service. Their own terms, privacy documentation, and configured regions may also apply. ConfessIt does not promise that all processing by every provider takes place in India.

## 7. Images and URL access

Profile pictures and love-note images are hosted by Cloudinary. Their URLs may be obscured or signed, but image access is not equivalent to a private, account-authenticated file vault. **Anyone who obtains a usable delivery URL may be able to view the image.** Do not upload highly sensitive, confidential, intimate, or otherwise private images that you would not want someone with the URL to see.

## 8. Chat encryption and limits

ConfessIt encrypts stored chat messages using AES-256-GCM. In production, the browser-to-public-edge connection is protected by TLS. Messages are inspected for content safety before encryption, and server-side components can decrypt messages for processing. ConfessIt is **not end-to-end encrypted**: the server can access the message content, and authorised service components may process it. Some internal service-to-service communications are not encrypted by default in the current configuration.

Chat encryption should not be understood as a guarantee that administrators, the operator, or a compromised service could never access message content. Avoid sending passwords, financial information, intimate information, or other highly sensitive data in chats.

## 9. Retention

Retention depends on the type of information and the reason it is held:

- **Matchmaking chat messages:** scheduled for deletion one day after the conversation ends.
- **Case Closed game-session chat/content:** deleted when the game session ends.
- **Confessions and comments:** are not automatically deleted when an account is deleted and may remain on the platform.
- **Moderation reports:** currently retained without a defined automatic deletion period and may be kept indefinitely for safety, security, abuse investigation, or legal reasons.
- **Operational and security logs:** currently retained without a defined automatic expiration. User-identifying information is hashed in logs where implemented, but hashing is not a guarantee of anonymisation.
- **Account and other service data:** retained for as long as needed to operate the service, administer accounts, handle safety issues, and meet applicable legal obligations, subject to applicable rights and deletion requests.

Retention practices may change as the service evolves. We will update this policy when material changes are made.

## 10. Account and data deletion requests

ConfessIt does not currently have a self-service account or personal-data deletion control. You may email [devathmaj@gmail.com](mailto:devathmaj@gmail.com) to request account closure or deletion of personal information. Requests are handled manually.

We will review and fulfil applicable requests, subject to legal obligations and legitimate safety or security needs. Where appropriate and technically feasible, we intend to delete or de-identify account-level personal information while allowing published confessions or comments to remain without a public link to the deleted account. Content is not automatically removed merely because an account is deleted. Moderation reports and records needed for safety or legal reasons may be retained.

Please identify the account or email address involved in your request, but do not send passwords, magic links, or other sign-in credentials by email. We may request reasonable information to verify that a request concerns your account.

## 11. Children and institutional accounts

ConfessIt's current minimum-age policy is 13, but the service does not collect a date of birth or independently verify users' ages. Organisations may provision accounts based on their own eligibility and cohort settings. An organisation and, where applicable, a parent or guardian should determine whether a young person may participate and obtain any permissions required by applicable law before an account is provisioned.

ConfessIt's minimum-age statement is not a representation that the current product has implemented every age-assurance, parental-consent, or child-safety requirement that may apply in every jurisdiction. If you are an organisation provisioning accounts, you should assess your own obligations before making the service available to minors.

## 12. Security

We use technical and operational measures intended to protect the service, including access controls, moderation workflows, security monitoring, transport encryption at the public edge in production, and encryption of stored chat messages. No online service or security measure can guarantee absolute security. As described above, ConfessIt is not end-to-end encrypted, some internal communications are not encrypted by default, and the handling of a URL-accessible image differs from private authenticated storage.

If you believe an account or personal information has been compromised, contact [devathmaj@gmail.com](mailto:devathmaj@gmail.com).

## 13. International use

ConfessIt is primarily operated from India but may be used internationally. Service providers may process information in the locations permitted by their service configuration and terms. Privacy and data-protection rights may vary by jurisdiction. Nothing in this policy is intended to remove rights that cannot lawfully be waived.
